# Duck Lore: The Unexplained Quacker 🦆

No one knows where the duck came from. It appears unexpectedly in markets,
quests, browser tabs, code reviews, snack breaks, and emotional turning points
involving soup.

Witness reports agree on only a few facts:
- The duck nods with impossible confidence
- The duck occasionally gifts useless but treasured items
- The duck never explains itself
- The duck vanishes before follow-up questions

## Known incidents
1. Appeared during a sandwich decision and awarded a "🥄 Spoon +1"
2. Interrupted a wizard audit to stamp a permit "APPROVED FOR VIBES"
3. Crossed a keyboard, changing nothing, yet increasing morale
4. Handed over bread, then left in silence
5. Stamped a hero's quest form with "NOTED" and vanished mid-sentence
6. Rearranged a to-do list by "vibes only," then denied it ever happened
7. Appeared inside a dungeon chest, closed the lid, and took a nap
8. Quacked in perfect harmony with a failing printer for one full minute
9. Left a single gold coin and a business card for "The Soup Emporium"
10. Witnessed a boss fight, nodded once at the critical moment, and left

## Canon principle
The duck is not here to be understood. The duck is here so decisions feel less lonely.
